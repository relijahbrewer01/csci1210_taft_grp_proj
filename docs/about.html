Who We Are

A Place to Belong. A Place to Grow.

At Taft Highway Church of Christ, we are a small, close-knit family of believers united by our love for God, His Word, and one another.

Our congregation brings together people of different ages and backgrounds with a common purpose: to worship God, grow in faith, and encourage one another in our walk with Christ.

You're Welcome Here

No matter your background, circumstances, or appearance, you are welcome here. We strive to create an atmosphere that is both comfortable and reverent, where everyone can experience genuine Christian love.

Come as you are. Grow with us.

Our Faith & Worship

Rooted in Scripture. Centered on Christ.

We believe that Jesus Christ is the head of His church and that Scripture is our ultimate authority in faith, worship, and Christian living. Our desire is to follow the teachings of Christ rather than the traditions of men.

We believe studying Scripture should inspire understanding, curiosity, and wonder. Through preaching, Bible classes, and thoughtful discussion, we encourage honest questions and a sincere pursuit of biblical truth.

Above all, we seek to honor God according to His Word, continually growing in our knowledge of Christ.

How We Worship Together

  • A cappella congregational singing
  • Heartfelt, reverent prayer
  • Biblical preaching and teaching
  • The Lord's Supper each Sunday morning, using unleavened bread and fruit of the vine in remembrance of Christ's body given for us and His shed blood.
  • Bible classes that encourage thoughtful questions, discussion, and spiritual growth

A Family Beyond Sunday

Our fellowship extends beyond the walls of our building. As a small congregation, we have the opportunity to know and care for one another personally.

Whether through prayer, encouraging cards, shared meals, Bible studies, or supporting those facing difficult times, we strive to demonstrate the love of Christ in everyday life.

We may be small in number, but our love for one another is not measured by our size.
